Our Quality

Quality and responsible sourcing, built in from the start

We work with trusted suppliers to bring quality food products to Hong Kong's premium restaurants and retailers, with sourcing decisions built around consistency and responsible supply.

Fresh Produce

Quality fruit and vegetables, sourced for consistency and freshness. We prioritise suppliers who can hold that standard against a fixed schedule, not just the lowest price.

Gourmet & Specialty

Specialty ingredients and pantry staples for premium menus, sourced in the smaller volumes a fine-dining kitchen actually needs, without bulk minimums.

Sustainability

We favour suppliers who manage stocks responsibly, and where possible, partners operating under recognised sustainability or environmental standards.

Quota-Managed Sourcing

Working within managed catch and supply limits, favouring suppliers under science-based quota systems over sourcing that outpaces what stocks can sustain.
