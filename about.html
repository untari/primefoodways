About Us

A direct line from trusted suppliers to Hong Kong's finest kitchens

Founded to bring quality food imports to Hong Kong's premium dining scene: fresh, traceable, reliable.

Our Approach

Small by design, not by accident

A focused, carefully chosen range, not a generic catalogue. That means direct supplier relationships, close quality control, and personal service over scale.

We're focused on fine-dining and premium restaurants, not chains or franchises.

Compliance & Credibility

Operating on the right footing, from day one

  • Hong Kong registered company. Incorporated via the Companies Registry.
  • Registered Food Importer & Distributor. Registered with Hong Kong's Centre for Food Safety.
  • Cold-chain freight partners. Perishables-focused forwarders, dock to storage.
  • Growing supplier network. Continually adding trusted suppliers to our range.
